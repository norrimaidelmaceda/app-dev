# The Princess Diaries

## Overview

*The Princess Diaries* is a **2001** comedy film directed by Garry Marshall and based on Meg Cabot's novel. It follows Mia Thermopolis, an awkward San Francisco teenager who learns she is the heir to the throne of the small European country of *Genovia*.

---

## Main Characters

- **Mia Thermopolis**: a shy high schooler turned reluctant princess
- **Queen Clarisse Renaldi**: Mia's grandmother, who trains her in princess lessons
- **Lilly Moscovitz**: Mia's loyal and outspoken best friend
- **Joe**: the queen's head of security and a quiet supporter of Mia
- *Lana Weinberger*: the popular girl who makes school difficult

## Mia's Princess Lessons

1. Learn proper posture and etiquette
2. Get a makeover
3. Master royal duties and public speaking
4. Decide whether to accept the crown
